ARC Requests

To change, add, or remove anything from the exterior or sides of your home, an ARC request is required.

Include the plat map and highlight or circle the area on the plat map where you are making changes or additions, along with any pictures.

You can submit it via the portal: https://cmp.cincwebaxis.com/

  • Architectural Request Form (DOCX)

Violations

Reporting Violations:

Please email: violations@hoapartner.com

Include a picture of the violation, along with a clear picture of the address you are reporting.

General Rules

  • Maintain lawns and landscaping.
  • No long term parking on the street.
  • No overnight parking in the pool parking lot.
  • Trash bins must be stored out of sight except on pickup day.
  • Trash should be properly secured and fit within the bins.
  • Architectural changes require HOA approval.
  • Do not allow people into the pool if they do not have a fob.
