Paddock Point Community Update!

Hello, neighbors!

We wanted to share a few exciting updates on what the Community Improvement, Events & Newsletter Committee has been working on behind the scenes!

NEWSLETTER & COMMUNITY UPDATES

As many of you have seen and used, Ivan previously created a website to help keep our community informed. This has been especially helpful for neighbors who may not be part of the Facebook page or community chat, giving everyone another way to stay updated on what’s happening around Paddock Point. We truly appreciate Ivan putting this together for our neighborhood!

COMMUNITY EVENTS

We’re currently working on a fun event for all of our neighborhood kiddos! We’re waiting on a couple of final details before officially setting the date, but our goal is to have the event sometime in September. More information will be shared as soon as everything is finalized — stay tuned!

COMMUNITY IMPROVEMENT PROJECTS

We also have two community improvement projects currently in development!

Dog Waste Bag Stations

We’re working on adding dog waste bag stations throughout the neighborhood. Our hope is to make it easier for everyone to clean up after their furry family members and help reduce dog waste being left in yards and common areas.

“Leave a Book, Take a Book” Stand

We’re also working on bringing a little community library to Paddock Point! Neighbors will be able to leave a book, take a book, or simply enjoy finding something new to read.

These projects take a little time to plan and coordinate, and we truly appreciate everyone’s patience while we work to bring them to life.

We’re excited about what’s ahead and look forward to continuing to find ways to bring our neighbors together and make Paddock Point an even better place to call home!

Thank you, neighbors!

— The Community Improvement, Events & Newsletter Committee

How to Report a Non-Emergency Safety Concern

If you observe concerning activity in the neighborhood, please use the following steps to make a clear report.

  1. For immediate danger or urgent situations, call 911.
  2. For non-emergency concerns, call the Spartanburg County non-emergency line at 864-596-2222.
  3. Provide the location and a brief factual description of what you observed.
  4. The non-emergency number connects you to an operator, who will route the call and dispatch the appropriate response.
  5. Depending on the situation, follow-up may be by phone, or an officer/deputy may be sent to your home if needed.
  6. If you have additional neighborhood concerns, you may also notify the HOA Board for documentation.
