HOA Elections

Meet the candidates running for the Paddock Point HOA Board.

Ivan Windon

I’m running for the HOA board because our neighborhood needs consistent rule enforcement, clear communication, and real transparency. Over the past few years, we’ve seen yards fall out of compliance and damage to shared spaces like the pool. These issues are fixable.

My background in technical operations, troubleshooting, and process management has taught me how to solve problems quickly, enforce standards fairly, and keep complex systems running smoothly. I’m committed to restoring consistency, improving communication, and ensuring decisions are handled openly and responsibly. I want our community to be a place we’re proud to live, and I’m ready to put in the work to make that happen.

Michael Walters

Many of you know me as the grass man. I’ve cut a lot of yards in this neighborhood and I care about keeping our community looking its best. I’m running for the HOA board because I believe this neighborhood can reach the high standard it was meant to have. In my year living here, I’ve seen yards not being maintained, pool rules ignored, and damage to shared areas. These issues affect everyone.

We need consistent and fair rule enforcement, clear communication about what’s happening in the community, and full transparency from the board. I’m committed to helping fix these problems and making sure our neighborhood stays clean, safe, and well-maintained.

David Kaplan

I am running for the Board President once again in the upcoming election. I largely agree with the feedback being shared about the Board over the past year, and I have a clear plan on how to improve our community. Now that obstacles are out of our way, it is time to focus on what a Board is truly supposed to do: improve the community at the grassroots level.

I have dedicated myself to, fought for, and served this community—often going well beyond what my position entails. I look forward to continuing to do so, not as I did a year ago, but as a wiser and more experienced president working for the continued betterment of our community.

Amy Crittenden

I am running to be a part of the board because I don’t want to sit on the sidelines and wish things were different in our neighborhood. I want to “BE THE CHANGE”. I feel that I can bring a fresh and different perspective to our neighborhood HOA because I have served on an HOA in the past and I know the work that it takes in the background.

What I would like to see in our little community is neighbors helping neighbors, like we did when Helene blew through. Or when you see someone that needs a helping hand, to simply be that helping hand.

Tyler Phelps

I never particularly wanted to serve on the HOA Board, but I believe our neighborhood needs change, and I am willing to step up and help. Professionally, I serve as the Associate Chief Information Officer for a local university, where I oversee budgets, teams, policy, governance, and organizational risk. I also previously worked as a Realtor, which gave me a strong understanding of how neighborhood appearance, maintenance, and reputation affect property values.

If elected, my priorities would be simple: fair and consistent enforcement of our existing covenants, responsible use of HOA funds, greater transparency and communication, and stronger accountability from our management company and vendors. I am not interested in creating more rules or micromanaging neighbors. I want us to take better care of the community we all invested in and make sure homeowners are getting the value and accountability they deserve. I would sincerely appreciate your support.

Rochea Jackson

My name is Rochea I have lived in Paddock Point for three years and I am excited to run for the HOA board. I want to contribute to the growth and success of our community. I am currently involved in the Pool Committee and attended monthly meetings. We have discussed and implemented changes to provide safety and clear instructions for pool usage.

Together we can create a community in Paddock Point that provides a safe and enjoyable community.

4th of July Firework Safety Reminder

Please celebrate responsibly and help keep our neighborhood safe.

  • Dangers: Keep children away from active fireworks and never relight a dud.
  • Safety: Use fireworks in open areas, keep water nearby, and clean up hot debris after use.
  • Damage: Be mindful of homes, cars, dry grass, and community property that can catch fire.
  • Decibels: Loud fireworks can impact neighbors, babies, veterans, and those sensitive to noise.
  • Dogs and Pets: Bring pets indoors early and use ID tags, as loud sounds can cause panic and escapes.
  • Hours: Per Spartanburg County regulations, fireworks can continue up until 1 am Sunday morning, July 5th, 2026.
  • Illegal types: M-80s, Cherry bombs, mini bottle rockets.
  • Responsibility: Celebrate respectfully, pick up all debris, and report unsafe situations when needed.

In an emergency, call 911. For non-emergency concerns, call the Spartanburg Sheriff's Department non-emergency line at 864-596-2222.
